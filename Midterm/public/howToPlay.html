How To Play

Idle Adventure

Idle adventure is a text based adventure game. The player can read and type their way through this fantasy world, exploring and fighting through challenges and adventures

The text that appears in the center of the screen will tell the player about what is happening in the world around them.

The right hand side of the screen will list any actions the player may take. These may include walking in a direction, or grabbing an item, or attacking an enemy

The left hand side of the screen is for equiping your character and seeing their stats
